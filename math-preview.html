This local reference page shows how to write equations for future posts.

Inline math

A normal random variable has expectation $\mathbb{E}[X]=\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$.

Displayed math

$$p(\theta\mid y)=\frac{p(y\mid\theta)\,p(\theta)}{\int p(y\mid\vartheta)\,p(\vartheta)\,d\vartheta}$$

Aligned equations

$$\begin{aligned} y_i &\sim \mathcal{N}(\alpha+\beta x_i,\sigma^2) \\ \alpha,\beta &\sim \mathcal{N}(0,10^2) \\ \sigma &\sim \operatorname{HalfNormal}(1) \end{aligned}$$

Writing a formula

<p>Inline: $\mathbb{E}[X] = \mu$.</p>
<div class="math-block">$$y = \alpha + \beta x$$</div>
